What did the brand see that everybody else missed?

An original 45-second series from Rhino House Media. We break down the decisions behind major campaigns, call out what worked or backfired, and pull out the move you can use in your own business.

That’s how we approach creative direction. Before we pitch an idea, we ask two questions.

What matters to your customer in this moment? What would give them a reason to act?

Those answers shape the strategy, the concept, and the work we produce. Your audience gets something worth paying attention to. Your brand gives them a reason to take the next step.

Campaigns & film

It could be the anticipation of that first sip of an ice-cold Corona. The confidence that comes with a pair of Ray-Bans. The pride of stepping out in fresh sneakers and wanting to keep them that way. In a feature film, it could be a single look that says what a character can’t.

At Rhino House Media, creative direction starts with understanding what you want your audience to feel. For brands, we find what makes a product matter to the person buying it and build the campaign around that. For producers, we take the feeling on the page and give it a visual language that carries from the opening frame to the final cut.
